What this solves

Quality gating

Score rough cuts and content segments with consistent thresholds so creators can reduce the time spent manually reviewing low-performing material.

Recommendation workflow

Suggest metadata improvements and editorial actions from profile-aware context, helping creators make decisions faster before upload time.

Submission confidence

Expose clear evidence for each generated recommendation and keep an audit trail for review and troubleshooting before sharing work.
